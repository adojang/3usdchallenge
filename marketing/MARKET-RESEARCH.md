# Market research: why PourPrice, why candle makers, why $3

## The brief
A product that can sell for **$3 per licence**, built and marketed with no further input.

At $3, the product has to be:
- **An impulse buy.** It solves one clear, painful problem that people already search for.
- **Cheap to support.** No servers, no accounts, no refunds caused by outages.
- **Sold into a community that already buys cheap digital tools.**

## Niches considered

| Idea | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Classroom seating-chart generator | ❌ Crowded with free tools (Shuffle Buddy, seatingchartmaker.app, Kuraplan, 101Planners, several AI tools). |
| Wedding seating planner | ❌ Free tools from WeddingWire and The Knot. It's also a one-off use, so there's no word of mouth after the wedding. |
| Generic habit, budget or invoice apps | ❌ Saturated, and $3 signals "low quality" there. |
| **Candle-maker calculator, costing and batch log** | ✅ **Chosen.** See below. |

## Evidence for the candle niche
- **People already pay for this, as spreadsheets.** Etsy has a whole search market for "candle calculator spreadsheet", "candle cost calculator" and "candle cost pricing". Listings sell all-in-one candle cost and pricing spreadsheets, and one claims to be "trusted by over 500 candle makers". Sources:
  - https://www.etsy.com/market/candle_calculator_spreadsheet
  - https://www.etsy.com/market/candle_cost_calculator
  - https://www.etsy.com/listing/927033472/all-in-one-candle-calculator-candle
- **The free options are fragmented.** Suppliers offer single calculators (fragrance load, cost). Craftybase gives away a pricing worksheet to sell its subscription inventory software. Nobody offers a cheap, one-time, all-in-one app that works on a phone. Sources:
  - https://craftybase.com/candle-pricing-worksheet
  - https://www.westsoundcandlesupply.com/pages/cost-calculator
- **Spreadsheets are awkward in this niche.** Makers weigh wax at the stove, often on a phone, with wax on their hands. Spreadsheet templates break easily, don't work well on phones, and don't track batches or cure dates.
- **The community is large and active.** There are candle-making subreddits, many large Facebook groups, and TikTok/Pinterest "candle business" content. A big share of hobbyists sell at markets or on Etsy, so they care about pricing.
- **The pain is emotional and about money.** "Am I charging enough?" is one of the most common questions small makers ask.

## Positioning
**PourPrice: know exactly what every candle costs, and what to charge.**
- Against spreadsheets: a real app, works on phone and desktop, nothing to break, has a batch log and stock tracking.
- Against subscription software (Craftybase and similar): a one-time $3 payment instead of a monthly fee, simpler, and private (no account, data never leaves the device).
- Against free single calculators: everything in one place, and it remembers your recipes.

## Pricing logic
$3 is below the price of competing Etsy spreadsheets. It's "less than a pack of wicks", so the buyer doesn't have to think about it. A free demo (2 recipes, 3 batches) removes the risk, and demo data can be imported into the full version, so the demo turns into sales.

**Recommendation:**
- **Payhip** as the main checkout. It keeps the most per sale and handles EU/UK VAT for you.
- **Etsy** as a second storefront. Buyers there are already searching for "candle cost calculator".

A higher price is possible later ($5–9 is still cheap next to the spreadsheets). $3 maximises volume and reviews at launch.
