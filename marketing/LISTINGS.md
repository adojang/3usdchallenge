## 1. Payhip / Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy product page

**Product name:** PourPrice – Candle Cost, Pricing & Batch Calculator

**Price:** $3 (one-time)

**Summary line / tagline:** Know exactly what every candle costs – and what to charge.

**Description:**

> **Stop guessing your candle prices.**
>
> PourPrice is a simple app for candle makers. It works out your wax and fragrance weights, your *true* cost per candle, and a profitable price. It also keeps a log of every batch you pour.
>
> **What it does**
> - ⚖️ **Pour calculator.** Get exact wax and fragrance oil weights for any number of candles, from your jar's water weight. Works in ounces or grams.
> - 🌸 **Fragrance blends.** Mix oils (e.g. 70% lavender / 30% vanilla). Measure fragrance load as % of wax or % of total. You get a warning if you go over your wax's maximum.
> - 💰 **True cost per candle.** Wax, oils, wicks, jars, lids, labels, boxes, your labour and overhead are all added up for you.
> - 🏷️ **Profitable pricing.** See wholesale and retail prices, your profit after Etsy/Shopify/market fees, and the price you need to hit your target margin.
> - 📒 **Batch book.** Record the lot number, temperatures, cure-ready date, cold and hot throw star ratings, and burn-test notes for each batch.
> - 📦 **Stock tracking.** Materials are deducted automatically when you log a batch, and you get a low-stock warning.
> - 🖨️ **Print pour sheets** for the workbench. **Export to CSV** for your records.
>
> **Why makers like it**
> - One-time $3. No subscription, no account, no sign-up.
> - Works offline. Your recipes and numbers never leave your device.
> - Works on computers (Windows, Mac, Chromebook) and on your phone as a home-screen app.
> - 20 currencies. Ounces or grams. Light and dark mode.
> - One-click backup and restore.
>
> **Try before you buy:** the free demo is at {{siteUrl}}/demo/. Anything you enter there can be imported into the full version.
>
> **You get:** a ZIP with the PourPrice app (opens in your browser), a quick-start guide, and a private link to install PourPrice on your phone or tablet. Free updates for version 1.x.
>
> *PourPrice gives estimates to help you plan. Always follow your suppliers' guidance and burn-test your candles.*
