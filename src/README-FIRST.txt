YOUR DATA
  - Everything you enter is stored only in the browser you use – nothing is uploaded.
  - Each browser/device keeps its own copy. To move data (e.g. computer -> phone):
    Settings -> "Export backup", then on the other device Settings -> "Import backup".
  - Clearing your browser's site data / history can erase it. Export a backup regularly.
  - Always open the file from the same place – if you move PourPrice.html to a
    different folder, some browsers treat it as a new app with empty data
    (just import your backup).

TIP: Click "Load example data" (Calculator or Settings) to explore first, then
     "Erase all data" in Settings when you're ready to enter your own.

HOW THE MATHS WORKS
  - Fill weight = vessel water weight x 0.86 (wax is lighter than water; adjustable).
  - Fragrance load defaults to "% of wax weight" (1 lb wax at 10% = 1.6 oz oil).
    You can switch to "% of total blend" in Settings.
  - Results are estimates. Always follow your wax/fragrance supplier's guidance
    (max fragrance load, flash point, temperatures) and burn-test your candles.
